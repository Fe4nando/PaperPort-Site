How it works

Three steps, one PDF.

No account setup, no digging through mirror sites, no manually stitching files together in another program.

01

Pick what you need

Level, subject, years, sessions, paper numbers and types — a guided picker walks you through it.

02

PaperPort does the rest

It checks several mirrors for each paper, verifies the file is real, and stamps on a cover page automatically.

03

Open one clean PDF

Every Question Paper you picked becomes one file, every Mark Scheme another — ready to print.

Features

Built to just work.

Every subject, every year

IGCSE and A-Level, going back as far as mirrors keep records — a simple level → subject → year → paper picker, no memorising syllabus codes.

Your files, your folder

Choose exactly where downloads land from Settings — nothing goes to a cloud account you don't control.

Sign in with Google

Used for one thing only: showing your real name in the sidebar. No ads, no analytics, no data collected beyond that.

Designed for non-technical users

Built for teachers and students first — a plain, guided interface, not a tool that assumes you're IT staff.
