Combine & Download

From "I need five years of 9709 Paper 2" to one file.

Three guided steps. No mirror sites, no renaming, no manual merging in a PDF tool.

1

Pick your level & subject

Choose IGCSE or A Level, then the subject. PaperPort knows every subject code and its display name.

2

Choose papers & sessions

Select the paper numbers, variants, exam sessions, and whether you want question papers, mark schemes, or both.

3

Download one combined PDF

Every question paper you picked is merged into one file, every mark scheme into another. Each one opens with a cover page.

Built to hand you a finished file, not a folder of PDFs.

Every subject, every year

IGCSE and A-Level, with a straightforward level → subject → year → paper picker.

One PDF per paper type

Every Question Paper you select gets merged into one file, every Mark Scheme into another — ready to print, no manual combining.

Cover page included

A clean cover page is added automatically to every paper — no setup, no template to pick.
