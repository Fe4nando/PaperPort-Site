PaperPort

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026

PaperPort is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server of its own, no user database, and does not track or analyse how you use it. This page explains the only two places any of your data touches something outside your computer: signing in with Google, and downloading past papers from third-party mirrors.

Signing in with Google

If PaperPort offers a "Sign in with Google" option, it uses Google's standard sign-in (OAuth 2.0) and requests only three pieces of information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo

This is used for exactly one thing: showing your real name and photo in the app's sidebar instead of your Windows username. PaperPort does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, or anything else on your Google account, and never asks for a password — Google's own sign-in page handles that, outside of PaperPort entirely.

Your sign-in session (an access token and a refresh token, so you don't have to sign in every time) is saved in a small settings file on your own computer and is never sent anywhere except back to Google to keep that session valid. Signing out, or uninstalling PaperPort, removes it.

Downloading past papers

When you download a paper, PaperPort connects your computer directly to one of a few third-party mirror websites to fetch that file. Those requests happen the same way any file download in a browser would — the mirror site sees a request from your device, the same as it would from anyone visiting it directly. PaperPort itself does not log, store, or transmit a record of what you've downloaded anywhere outside your own computer.

What PaperPort does not do

  • No analytics, advertising IDs, or usage tracking of any kind
  • No account or database on any server — everything lives on your computer
  • No selling or sharing of any data, because none is collected to share

Where your data lives

Entirely on your own computer: your downloaded papers in the folder you choose (see Settings), and your Google session in PaperPort's local settings folder. Nothing is uploaded by PaperPort at any point.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Continuing to use PaperPort after an update means you accept the current version.
